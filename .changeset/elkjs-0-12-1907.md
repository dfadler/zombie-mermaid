Support elkjs 0.12.0. The optional `elkjs` peer range of `@zombie-mermaid/svg-renderer` widens from `^0.11.0` to `^0.11.0 || ^0.12.0`, so both versions keep working; layouts are identical on both. `zombie-mermaid` (CLI) now depends on `elkjs@^0.12.0`. Closes [#1907](https://github.com/dfadler/zombie-mermaid/issues/1907).
