'zombie-mermaid': patch
---

Output change, ASCII: a lone Z route now bends at the middle of its gap, as mermaid@12.3.0 bends it, in a top-down or bottom-up diagram, and for the strokes out of a hub in a left-to-right or right-to-left one (where the layout bends it mid-gap). The fan-in strokes into a node and the fan-out strokes out of it used to bend at a third or two thirds of the gap.
