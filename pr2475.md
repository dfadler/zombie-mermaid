## What changed

SVG flowchart (and state/C4/architecture, which share the marker) arrowheads now have a fixed size instead of scaling with the edge's stroke width.

- Markers get `markerUnits="userSpaceOnUse"`, so the head is the same at 1px, thick and `linkStyle stroke-width:3px`.
- Head is 10.5 long x 12.8 wide (was 8x5 at 1px). The issue says 8x8, but that is mermaid's unused `pointEnd` marker; edges actually use `pointEnd-margin` (viewBox 11.5x14 in a 10.5x14 box, so 10.5 x 12.8), confirmed by a screenshot of mermaid 12.2.0.
- Thick (`==>`) edges are 3.5px (was 2px).
- Normal edges stay 1px.

## Widths: what I chose and why

Measured with mermaid 12.2.0 (the repo pin and npm latest) through `scripts/lib/real-mermaid.ts`, computed styles on `flowchart-link` paths: normal `1px`, thick `3.5px`, dotted dash `2 2`. The audit comment on #1592 said mermaid's normal width is 2px; the computed style in the headless render is 1px (`.edge-thickness-normal{stroke-width:1px}`), so our 1px already matches and only thick changes.

## Baselines

SVG gallery baselines containing an arrowhead marker were regenerated on darwin (67 PNGs). The default Playwright tolerance (`maxDiffPixelRatio` 0.002) hides the change on small diagrams, so I deleted the affected darwin files and rewrote them rather than relying on `--update-snapshots`. Linux baselines are CI-generated and are committed to this branch from the CI artifacts. Unrelated darwin drift in pie/xychart baselines was left untouched.
